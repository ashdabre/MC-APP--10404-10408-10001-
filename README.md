## 📌 Overview

**What's In My Fridge?** is a smart, AI-powered mobile application designed to help users reduce food waste and simplify meal planning. The app allows users to scan their fridge by either **taking a photo** or **uploading an image** of the fridge interior. It leverages **advanced image recognition** and **machine learning** to identify food items in real time and suggest recipes accordingly.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- 📸 **Fridge Scanning**  
  Upload or capture a photo of your fridge. The app automatically detects and lists all visible food items using AI-powered image analysis.

- 🍲 **Smart Recipe Suggestions**  
  Based on the identified ingredients, the app provides real-time recipe suggestions that can be prepared instantly.

- ❗ **Missing Ingredient Alerts**  
  If a suggested recipe requires an ingredient that’s not currently available, the app highlights it and suggests alternatives or additions.

- 🛒 **Instant Grocery Ordering**  
  Seamlessly order any missing ingredients through integrated delivery partners like **Zepto** or **Blinkit** with a single tap.

---

## ✅ Benefits

- Minimize **food waste** by utilizing what's already in your fridge.
- **Save time** thinking about what to cook every day.
- Enjoy a **streamlined cooking and shopping experience**.
- Great for **busy professionals**, **students**, and **families** looking to optimize food usage.

---

## 💡 Use Case Example

You upload a photo of your fridge containing:
- Tomatoes  
- Bell Peppers  
- Cheese  
- Eggs  

The app might suggest:
- **Cheesy Veg Omelette**
- **Stuffed Bell Peppers**

If a recipe requires **bread** and it's not detected, the app will:
- Alert you about the missing ingredient.
- Offer a one-tap option to order it via Zepto or Blinkit.
